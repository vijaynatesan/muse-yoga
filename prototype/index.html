Chakrasana

Public Library · Submitted by Priya

AdvancedBack & SpineBackbendCharacter: Boy

Benefits

Strengthens arms, wrists, legs and spine. Stretches chest, lungs, abdomen and hip flexors. Increases spinal flexibility and energy.

⚠ Cautions — do not practise if you have: back or neck injury · wrist injury or carpal tunnel · high/low blood pressure · heart conditions · pregnancy.

These steps and images are stored once and reused in every practice. They are never regenerated, and nothing here can be typed over — corrections go through Feedback only.

Prototype note: step images and text shown are the Chakrasana sample set for every asana in this mock.

Steps · 4 steps · shown in coaching exactly as here

Step 1 · 20s

Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat and hip-width apart. Arms relaxed by your sides.

Step 2 · 15s

Palms flat beside your ears, fingers toward shoulders. Feet parallel, knees over ankles.

Step 3 · 15s

Press into your feet and lift hips, then press into hands to lift head and chest.

Step 4 · 30s

Straighten arms and legs into Wheel Pose. Breathe steadily, gaze between your hands.
